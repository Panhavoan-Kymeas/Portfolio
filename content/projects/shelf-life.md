## About

Shelf Life is a reading log focused on quotes, questions, and connections between books. It generates a static personal library that is easy to own and publish.

## What I learned

Content-heavy interfaces benefit from a strong information hierarchy more than extra features. Thoughtful typography did more for this project than another round of functionality.
